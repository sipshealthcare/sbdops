## What it is

Adding a staff member by hand offers White Belt as the only option, so anyone not yet assessed is
recorded as White. This is the same defect class as the placeholder belt on the report: a default
printed where a decision belongs.

## Why it matters

56 of 92 staff sit at White and 28 of those are still flagged as needing placement, so roughly half
the White population has not been assessed at White. That distorts every roster view and every
report grouped by belt.

## What is known

The belt field already holds a non-belt value on 13 records, so the data side may be lighter than
the interface side.

## Before building

A written impact note is wanted first: what an unassessed state touches in belt progress, the
reports, the assessment queue, and how the 28 existing records get corrected.
