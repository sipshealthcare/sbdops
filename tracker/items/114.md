## What it is

A staff admin opens the Assessment Queue and sees nothing, while requests exist in the database.
The queue renders only in the admin view, so the account holds the right and has no door to walk
through.

## Why it matters

Same shape as the assessor console issue closed 2026-07-30, where a granted right existed with no
screen behind it. A capability that cannot be reached is not a capability.

## How to check it when it ships

Sign in as a staff admin, open Assessment Queue, confirm the open requests appear and can be
actioned within that account's facility scope.
