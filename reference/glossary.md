# Glossary

Shared vocabulary. Prevents the class of error where a heading reads SBD when it means SPD.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SPD** | Sterile Processing Department. The hospital department. |
| **SBD** | Sterile By Design. The SIPS methodology and product family. Not interchangeable with SPD. |
| **SBD PSOP** | Process Standard Operating Procedures. The Process component. |
| **SBD WIP** | Workforce Intelligence and Performance. The People component. This platform. |
| **SBD OTIS** | Operational Tracking Intelligence System. The Work Flow component. |
| **Belt** | What an operator can do. White, Yellow, Green, Blue, Brown, Black. |
| **OIP** | Operator Identity Profile. How someone leads and learns, as distinct from what they can do. Four types: Sentinel, Steward, Surgeon, Architect. |
| **Gate** | One of the three checks: Competency, Simulation, Observation. All three gate independently. |
| **Conditional** | A belt awarded with conditions attached. Not a failure and not a clean pass. |
| **Knowledge Foundation** | Cleared the knowledge bar but not the blended threshold. Not the same as White. |
| **Provision** | A condition sitting on a person's account, most often a patient safety finding, holding them from the next belt until cleared. |
| **Free agent** | Released from a facility, held in the registry with belt, stars and reason for leaving. |
