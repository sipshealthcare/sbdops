# Severity

Generic P1 to P4 does not fit a system that certifies competency in sterile processing. A scoring
defect here is not a normal bug. Grade on consequence.

| Tier | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Record integrity.** A belt, gate or record is wrong, or a safety finding is missed | White substituted as a placeholder belt and printed as a result |
| 2 | **Silent data loss.** Reports success, stores nothing | Schedule and attendance saving nothing for two months |
| 3 | **Access and exposure.** Someone sees or changes what they should not | Five areas readable by anyone signed in |
| 4 | **Blocked work.** Someone cannot do a task they are required to do | An assessor holding the right with no screen to use it |
| 5 | **Wrong display.** Data is correct underneath, reads wrong on screen | "Not gated" printing as a blank |
| 6 | **Suggestion.** Not broken, wanted | Endoscopy modules, scripts as a standalone module |

Tiers 1 and 2 reach Ignacio outside the batch. Everything else waits for the next triage packet.

Submitters never set severity. They report what happened and how much it is in their way. Severity
is assigned at triage, because an administrator has no way to know that "the belt looks wrong" is a
tier 1 and "this page is slow" is not.
