# Capability register

**What the platform does today.** Present tense only. Confirmed live. Safe to say out loud to a
client or a prospect.

This is not the roadmap and not the backlog. If something is not live, it belongs in
`limitations.md` instead.

## Assessment and placement

Placement assessment scores knowledge and simulation against per-belt floors and a 60/40 blend,
following Scoring Specification v1. Four outcomes: Clean, Conditional, Knowledge Foundation, No
Belt. A No Belt result can be approved without certifying the person at White.

Dangerous answers raise a patient safety provision that sits on the person's account, holds them
from the next belt until a master admin or SIPS admin clears it, and appears as the first condition
on their report. Cleared entries keep the name and date.

## Three gate model

Competency, Simulation and Observation each gate separately. Observation runs on the floor with a
two-PIN handshake, typed or spoken evidence captured per item, and a Stop Work control. An item
with no evidence written does not count as answered, so an observation cannot be passed by
selecting from a list.

## Learning

Foundations across ten modules carrying full source content, 77 sections. Instruments. Position
Schools. Preceptor certification with proctored gates. A knowledge or simulation test completes
after three passes at 80 percent or higher, with fresh randomised questions on every attempt.
Observation unlocks only after three passes of each.

## Operations

Scheduling and attendance per facility with a publish gate, so staff see a schedule only once it is
published. Attendance supports present, absent, late, coverage, PTO and excused. Transfers and free
agent handling. Role management with per-facility assessor rights, enforced at the database.

## DAVID

Per-facility metering with monthly limits, warnings, a hard stop and a global off switch. Usage
split by application. Assessment grading cost tracked separately from the chat allowance. Model
fallback so a provider outage yields a graded result rather than an error.
